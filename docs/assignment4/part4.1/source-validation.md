## Volume findings

For BTC every message carried day_volume=last_size=vol_24hr=vol_all_currencies=41377222656. For ETH all four fields were 14926891008. They stayed unchanged as prices changed. These are snapshot/24h-like quantities, not incremental trade sizes. In particular this sample does not support treating last_size as last-trade volume.

Yahoo's market table labels volume-in-currency and total-all-currencies over 24 hours. That supports a 24h snapshot interpretation, but neither the inspected protobuf schema nor this sample establishes exact units/aggregation/window rules for each stream field. currency=USD is explicit for quote price; it does not itself prove each volume field's unit. Do not label these as BTC/ETH units, daily monotone counters, incremental volume or exact traded minute volume. Do not sum or difference them. Candle volume stays null.

## Identity assessment and implemented decision

Legitimate distinct updates CAN share a whole-second source timestamp; absence of a source ID/sequence means timestamps cannot prove uniqueness. The six-message sample happened to have no repeated same-symbol timestamp, so distinct same-time updates were tested with explicitly fictional fixtures, not claimed as observed source corrections.

The previous replacement heuristic could erase an observed high/low. For yahoo_stream provenance, current policy is:
- preserve every receipt;
- exact canonical repeat of the latest payload in the same base slot is an app duplicate, referencing its last version (not proof of retransmission or trade identity);
- a different same-slot payload creates a NEW application UUID/receipt-disambiguated quote contribution with ambiguity flag, revision_no=1 and no supersedes link;
- A->B->A creates three contributions, retaining highs/lows and latest known close in stable receipt tie order;
- retain fixture-only replacement revisions for regression of the explicit app-revision mechanism. They are not evidence Yahoo supplies correction IDs.

The legacy identity_kind label app_quote_slot remains an application label. New Yahoo slot_key includes app receipt sequence; base_slot_key groups potential collisions. Schema v2 adds this grouping column/index and policy marker. v1 fixture-only databases migrate additively without altering receipts/versions/candles; v1 databases containing Yahoo receipts stop for explicit identity-history review rather than silently reinterpret old history. No working DB was migrated.

Replay reads accepted versions ONLY through the requested receipt cutoff and applies them sequentially. Tests verify both explicit fixture revision history and Yahoo append-preservation: future versions never change earlier replay states. All current source-volume projections remain unavailable.
